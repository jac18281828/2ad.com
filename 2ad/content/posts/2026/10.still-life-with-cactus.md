A still life path-traced by [gman]({filename}/pages/gman.md): a gold ball on a
pedestal, a glass ball, a potted prickly pear, a mirror ball and a blue torus on
a marble floor before a clay wall. A warm disk overhead and a small blue lamp
light it, and the path tracer follows every bounce between them, 2,048 paths a
pixel at 1280 by 800, in about eleven minutes on an Apple M3 Max.

Everything in the frame is generated. One Python script paints the cream and
slate marble; another writes the cactus's 16-bit skin and builds the cactus as a
RIB archive of four pads, each areole a green cushion with a white tuft and a
straw spine or two. The glass ball is a lens: look into it and the pot and the
cactus stand upside down.

The scene, the scripts and a `make hq` target that renders it at 1920 by 1200
are in [still-life-material](https://github.com/jac18281828/still-life-material).
gman is at [github.com/jac18281828/gman](https://github.com/jac18281828/gman).
