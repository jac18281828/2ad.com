## Try it

Install gman as Get it on the [gman page](/gman.html) shows, then, from the unpacked folder:

```sh
gman -r gmanraytracer samples/vase.rib
```

That writes `vase.png`, the picture above, in a few seconds. The gman page carries the rest: the path tracer, the radiosity pass and a first scene from nothing.

## From exit 1 to a ray tracer

At the start of the revival, `gman sphere.rib` exited 1 with `TOKEN_NOT_FOUND` and produced no file. By 0.9.1 the README opened on the ray-traced vase above.

[John: arc — the revival in your words: what the first weeks were like, and what surprised you between the z-buffer and the ray tracer.]

- **0.6.0, 2026-09-06.** The first release in 24 years. It renders the seven quadrics and convex `Polygon`, shaded by `matte`, `plastic` or `metal` and written out as TIFF, PNG, JPEG or PNM.
- **0.7.0, 2026-09-07.** Anti-aliasing: `PixelSamples` and `PixelFilter` reach the renderer. `Patch` and `PatchMesh` render.
- **0.7.1, 2026-09-08.** Parameter arrays carry the length the parser read, which closes an out-of-bounds heap read on malformed RIB that affected 0.6.0 and 0.7.0.
- **0.8.0, 2026-09-17.** Texturing through `MakeTexture` and `texture()`, environment maps, `spotlight`, and `GeneralPolygon`, `PointsPolygons` and `NuPatch`.
- **0.8.1, 2026-09-17.** Groundwork for the ray tracer: `GMANRay` carries an origin, a normalized direction and a `[tmin, tmax]` interval.
- **0.8.2, 2026-09-19.** gman installs as a CMake package a program finds with `find_package(gman)`.
- **0.8.3, 2026-09-22.** The ray tracer renders, builds by default and installs: `gman -r gmanraytracer scene.rib`.
- **0.9.0, 2026-09-23.** `mirror` and `glass` join the shader plugins, tracing real reflection and refraction.
- **0.9.1, 2026-09-24.** The ray tracer draws `GeneralPolygon`, `PointsPolygons` and `PointsGeneralPolygons`, holes included.

## Poke it

Change one line of `samples/vase.rib` and render it again with `gman -r gmanraytracer samples/vase.rib`. Each picture starts from the original scene.

| Picture | Edit |
|---|---|
| ![the vase in glass]({static}/images/2026/gman-glass.png) | **Glass vase.** In the `## Vase` block, make the surface `Surface "glass"` and delete the `Opacity`. |
| ![the robot's dome as a mirror]({static}/images/2026/gman-mirror.png) | **Mirror dome.** Under `# head dome`, make the surface `Surface "mirror" "Kr" [1]`. |
