## Try it

```sh
curl -sL https://github.com/jac18281828/gman/releases/download/0.9.1/gman-0.9.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz | tar xz
curl -sLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jac18281828/gman/0.9.1/samples/vase.rib
export PATH="$PWD/gman-0.9.1-linux-x86_64/bin:$PATH"
gman -r gmanraytracer vase.rib
```

That writes the picture above. Linux arm64 and macOS arm64 builds are on the [releases page](https://github.com/jac18281828/gman/releases); the [GMAN page](/gman.html) has more.

## From exit 1 to a ray tracer

[John: the arc, in your words. Facts from the changelog, one release at a time — keep, cut or merge.]

When the revival began on 2026-08-15, `gman sphere.rib` exited 1 with `TOKEN_NOT_FOUND` and wrote no file.

- **0.6.0 (09-06) — it renders.** The first release in 24 years. The seven quadrics and convex `Polygon`, shaded matte, plastic or metal, written as TIFF, PNG, JPEG or PNM. CMake replaced autotools. Fixes that made it work: array parameters such as `fov [45]` were silently dropped, transform composition was backwards, and perspective clipping cut 37% of every wide frame.
- **0.7.0 (09-07) — antialiasing and patches.** `PixelSamples` and `PixelFilter` had been parsed and discarded since 1999. `Patch` and `PatchMesh` render, bilinear and bicubic. Two of the five pixel filters were wrong, and nothing had ever called them.
- **0.7.1 (09-08) — a security fix.** A malformed `Patch` could read past a heap allocation.
- **0.8.0 (09-17) — texturing.** `MakeTexture` and `texture()`, environment maps and `shinymetal`, `spotlight`, `GeneralPolygon` with holes, `PointsPolygons`, `NuPatch`. The 1999 pthread wrapper is gone.
- **0.8.2 (09-19) — a real library.** gman installs as a CMake package, `find_package(gman)` works, and a release build exports 793 symbols instead of 1604.
- **0.8.3 (09-22) — the ray tracer renders.** `gman -r gmanraytracer` traces spheres, disks, polygons and the quadrics, with shadow rays and layered transparency.
- **0.9.0 (09-23) — reflection and refraction.** `mirror` and `glass` shaders through a new `trace()` call, `Torus`, a BVH so the tracer scales past a linear scan, and a `fresnel()` that returns physical values.
- **0.9.1 (09-24) — the rest of the polygons.** The ray tracer draws `GeneralPolygon`, `PointsPolygons` and `PointsGeneralPolygons`; the z-buffer dices polygons by screen size, taking a 10,000-quad render from 3.53 s to 0.61 s.
