This book brings the Punic Wars down to one morning on a North African plain. It weighs Hannibal
against Scipio, from the Alps and Cannae to the day they met at Zama.

### Zama, 202 BCE

Hannibal's edge had always been his cavalry. At Cannae his horsemen closed the trap behind the
Roman army. At Zama the best of them rode for Scipio.

Hannibal brought eighty war elephants, more than he had ever led. Scipio set his maniples one
behind another, leaving open lanes through his ranks. Hannibal sent the elephants in first. At
the blast of Roman trumpets, some of them wheeled and stampeded back through his own Numidian
cavalry.
