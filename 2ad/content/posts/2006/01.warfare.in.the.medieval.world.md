Years' War. Carey follows the same four weapon systems while heavy cavalry rises, rules the

### Manzikert, 1071 CE

By the late 1060s the Seljuk Turks were moving into Byzantine Anatolia. Their horse archers wore
little armor and fought with the short composite bow, striking from a distance and retreating
when charged, sometimes for days. Byzantine doctrine answered them with light infantry bowmen
beside the cavalry.

In the summer of 1071 the emperor Romanus IV Diogenes gathered his army at Erzurum to
retake Manzikert. The sultan Alp Arslan, at Aleppo, turned his army around and marched
for the frontier. Romanus had sent his light infantry to a siege elsewhere.

On 19 August Romanus advanced with his heavy cavalry in a single broad line. The Turkish center
fell back before him while the wings showered his flanks with arrows. As evening came, he
ordered the army back to camp, and his second line, under Andronicus Ducas, rode off.

> **And the emperor was left to the Turks.**
