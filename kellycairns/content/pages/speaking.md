Dr. Kelly Cairns is a board-certified veterinary internal medicine specialist and nationally recognized speaker on veterinary education, leadership, professional wellbeing and clinical medicine. She speaks at conferences, colleges of veterinary medicine, veterinary hospitals and industry events. Her audiences include veterinarians, technicians, students and leaders across the profession. Every talk is built around the audience in the room.

## Speaking areas

### Veterinary education {: style="margin-bottom:0.3em; font-weight:600"}
How veterinary professionals are trained and developed, from first-year student to experienced clinician: curriculum design, competency-based education, clinical reasoning and the transition to practice.

* Reimagining Veterinary Education: An Integrated Model
* From Blueprint to Classroom: Practical Lessons in Veterinary Curriculum Development and Implementation

### Leadership, mentorship and medical excellence {: style="margin-bottom:0.3em; font-weight:600"}
Building the people, systems and culture behind high-quality veterinary care, in single hospitals and across national networks.

* Effective Mentorship: Core Principles for All Practice Settings
* Getting Buy-In, in the Exam Room and Beyond

### Purpose and professional wellbeing {: style="margin-bottom:0.3em; font-weight:600"}
Sustaining passion, purpose and resilience in a demanding profession, for individuals and teams. Rekindling the Spark is her signature keynote, delivered at Fetch dvm360, VCA Canada and Veterinary Growth Partners.

* Rekindling the Spark: Sustaining Passion and Purpose from the Heart
* Lessons From My Tech Teachers: Our Call to Action

### Small animal internal medicine {: style="margin-bottom:0.3em; font-weight:600"}
Practical, case-based continuing education, from single lectures to full-day programs.

* Demystifying Feline Liver Disease
* Canine and Feline Chronic Enteropathy
* Addison's Disease: When to Look and How to Act
* Chronic Kidney Disease: A Fresh Look at an Old Disease

*Other popular topics include the icteric cat, canine chronic hepatitis, Cushing's disease, polyarthritis, pancreatitis, triaditis, cardiorenal syndrome, platelet disorders and steroids in the cardiac patient. Ask about any topic not listed here.*
