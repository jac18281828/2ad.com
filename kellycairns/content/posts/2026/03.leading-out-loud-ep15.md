Leadership in veterinary medicine is about more than medicine. It is about people, systems, mentorship and the environments that help teams thrive. On *Leading Out Loud*, a podcast about leadership in healthcare settings, host Bridget Rollins talks with Dr. Kelly Cairns about her journey from clinician to leader and what it takes to build medical excellence across the veterinary profession.
