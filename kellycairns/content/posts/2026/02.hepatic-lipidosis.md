Dr. Kelly Cairns' article *Moving the Needle: Early Intervention in Feline Hepatic Lipidosis* has been accepted for publication in *The Feline Practitioner* and is expected in Winter 2026. The RACE-approved continuing education article focuses on early intervention in hepatic lipidosis, one of the most common liver diseases in cats.
