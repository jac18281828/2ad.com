*Keynote, Viticus Group Annual Veterinary Technician Symposium, October 17, 2022 (49 minutes)*

In this keynote, Dr. Cairns shares what the veterinary technicians she has worked alongside taught her, and how those lessons still shape the way she teaches and leads. It is a call to action for technicians to recognize what they alone bring to the profession.

She gave the keynote at the Viticus Group Annual Veterinary Technician Symposium on October 17, 2022, and also presented it in the Penn Foster Veterinary Academy Webinar Series in February 2022.
