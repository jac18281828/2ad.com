On episode 226 of *So You're a Vet... Now What?*, host Dr. Moriah McCauley talks with Dr. Kelly Cairns, a board-certified small animal internal medicine specialist, about the journey from nervous veterinary student to confident veterinarian and leader.

Dr. Cairns shares what she has learned about helping new graduates thrive, and why trust built on genuine human relationships is at the heart of every strong veterinary team. She explains how leaders can create an environment where team members feel comfortable and valued, starting with getting to know each person as an individual.
