Most new kittens come home already weaned. But what if you find a kitten who has been abandoned before it is old enough to leave its mother? In this episode of *19 Cats and Counting*, Dr. Kelly Cairns joins host Rita Reimers to talk about caring for found kittens.

![Kelly Cairns](/static/KellyCairns_thumb.jpg){: height=100 style="float:right; padding:16px"}

Dr. Cairns covers what very young kittens need, how often they should be fed and when to bring them to the veterinarian, so you are prepared to give a foundling kitten the best possible start in life.
