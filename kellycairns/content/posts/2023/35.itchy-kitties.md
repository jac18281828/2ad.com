When is scratching more than just an itch? In this episode of *19 Cats and Counting*, Dr. Kelly Cairns joins host Rita Reimers to talk about cats who scratch until they leave scabs and raw sores, and why that is always a reason to see a veterinarian.

![Kelly Cairns](/static/KellyCairns_thumb.jpg){: height=100 style="float:right; padding:16px"}

Dr. Cairns walks through what might, and might not, be behind a cat's itchiness, what your veterinarian will be looking for and how to get an uncomfortable cat back to feeling like themselves.
