Senior cats often end up in shelters through no fault of their own, when an owner passes away or moves into a care facility. In this episode of *19 Cats and Counting*, Dr. Kelly Cairns joins host Rita Reimers, who has adopted several senior cats herself, to talk about what these older cats need.

![Kelly Cairns](/static/KellyCairns_thumb.jpg){: height=100 style="float:right; padding:16px"}

Dr. Cairns explains the special needs of aging cats and how to prepare to care for a cat who is getting up in years, and why none of it should stand in the way of opening your home to a senior cat.
